# Licensing agentes.chat

agentes.chat comes under two licenses. You pick the one that fits.

## The AGPL (free)

The code in this repository is licensed under the [GNU AGPL-3.0-only](LICENSE), with the terms in
[NOTICE](NOTICE): a permission to distribute the phone and desktop apps through app stores, and the
attribution "Based on agentes.chat by Codari Global, LLC" kept in the About screen. A few packages
say they are MIT in their own LICENSE file.

Under it you can, at no charge and without asking:

- run agentes.chat on your own server and use it in your company, for as many people as you like;
- change the code;
- install and run it for clients and charge for your work;
- offer a changed version to others, as long as you publish your changes under the AGPL and show
  its users where to get the source code.

The AGPL always asks you to keep the license and the attribution, and the agentes.chat name and
logo stay ours ([TRADEMARKS.md](TRADEMARKS.md)): a fork needs a name, a logo and apps of its own.

## The commercial license

Codari Global, LLC also licenses agentes.chat on commercial terms, by contract, for those who:

- change it and offer it to others without publishing their changes;
- sell it under their own brand, without the attribution;
- build it into a closed product;
- work for a company that doesn't allow AGPL software.

There is no license key: the code is the same under both licenses.

## Questions

Not sure which one you need? Write to legal@agentes.chat.

Contributions to this repository come in under our [Contributor License Agreement](CLA.md), which is
what lets us offer both ([ADR 0180](docs/adr/0180-dual-licensing-and-cla.md)).
